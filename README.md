## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this analysis is to explore trends in the Steam gaming market, understand player preferences, identify popular genres and publishers, and uncover actionable business insights for game developers and publishers.[cite: 1] 

By analyzing pricing, reviews, hardware requirements, and platform trends, this project provides a data-driven blueprint for optimal release strategies and market positioning.[cite: 1]

---

## 📊 Key Business Insights & Findings

* **Market Saturation & The "Golden Formula":** Most of the market is crowded with cheap titles under $10.[cite: 1] However, the majority of the estimated revenue is generated by games priced between $30 and $60.[cite: 1] Blending Action and RPG mechanics with Multiplayer/Co-op features justifies premium pricing and maximizes returns.[cite: 1]
* **Strategic Release Timing:** October and November are highly congested as developers rush to launch before the Steam Winter Sale.[cite: 1] Releasing in Q1 or Q2 offers better visibility for smaller studios.[cite: 1] Additionally, Tuesday and Thursday dominate as the preferred days to launch games, aligning with press cycles and Steam's algorithm.[cite: 1]
* **Player Sentiment & Pricing Risk:** Paid games usually score better in reviews, while Free-to-Play games are highly vulnerable to review bombing.[cite: 1] Furthermore, as prices approach the $60 AAA tier, variance in positive reviews widens, indicating players are much more unforgiving of bugs at premium prices.[cite: 1] Early Access games often enjoy slightly higher positivity as players are more lenient with works-in-progress.[cite: 1]
* **Hardware Reality Check:** Minimum RAM requirements have moved on a strict upward trajectory.[cite: 1] The industry standard shifted from a 2GB baseline in 2010 to an average minimum requirement of 7GB by 2024.[cite: 1] Optimizing for older 4GB systems is no longer a viable use of development resources.[cite: 1]
* **Genre Dominance:** While "Singleplayer" dominates user tags, and "Action", "Indie", and "Adventure" are the most saturated genres, it is the "Action" and "RPG" genres that dominate actual revenue.[cite: 1]

## 🛠️ Data Processing & Methodology

To prepare the raw Steam marketplace data for analysis, the following data cleaning and feature engineering steps were executed:

1. **Data Cleaning:** Handled missing values by dropping records with missing release dates and genres, and filled missing Developer/Publisher information with "Unknown".[cite: 1] Removed irrelevant product types to focus strictly on `game` and `dlc` categories.[cite: 1]
2. **Text Normalization:** Extracted numerical RAM values from messy text strings and wrote a custom normalization function utilizing regular expressions (`re`) to standardize wildly varying Operating System requirements.[cite: 1]
3. **Feature Engineering:** Created robust analytical features including:[cite: 1]
   * `TotalReviews` and `Pos_Ratio` to measure engagement and player sentiment.[cite: 1]
   * `ReleaseYear`, `ReleaseMonth`, and `ReleaseDay` for time-series and seasonality analysis.[cite: 1]
   * `Rev_Proxy` (Price × Total Reviews) to estimate relative commercial impact.[cite: 1]
   * Boolean flags like `IsFree`, `Is_EA` (Early Access), `Is_Multi`, and `Is_Indie` (Self-Published) to segment the market.[cite: 1]
